## Final Goal

Build production-oriented AI systems that can:

1. Collect operational data.
2. Analyze logs, metrics, events, and traces.
3. Correlate multiple signals.
4. Investigate incidents.
5. Identify probable root causes.
6. Recommend remediation.
7. Execute approved actions safely.
8. Generate incident reports and postmortems.

**Learning approach:** Understand → Implement → Test → Document → Build projects.
